Color Eightball (or Rainbow Launcher) is an Unreal Tournament (1999) mutator that replaces the regular rocket launcher with a rocket launcher that shoots randomly colored balls with different effects. Effects include: freeze, fat explosion, redeemer, regular missile, disintegrate, lava, teleport, shockwave, and black hole. Available as a regular mutator and as Arena.

There are 9 random effects that will spawn out of the Color Eightball launcher:

Blue Projectile - This projectile just does normal rocket damage.
Green Projectile - This projectile will disintegrate the victim and spawn goo.
Yellow Projectile - This projectile will cause the victim to increase in fatness until they explode.
White Projectile - This projectile will freeze the victim and leave an ice statue in place.
Red Projectile - This projectile is a redeemer from the game.
Purple Projectile - This projectile is a redeemer shockwave with no damage - pushes players around.
Lava Projectile - This projectile is a lava ball that spawns lava splash.
Teleport Projectile - This projectile will teleport you to a random location in the map.
Black Hole Projectile - This will launch and spawn a black hole for pure chaos.


There is a menu setting to flag redeemer on and off.
